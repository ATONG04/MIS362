Assignment Purpose and Learning Outcomes

The goal of this assignment is to examine how organizational culture, change, leadership, employee participation, and career requirements influence the success of information systems.

After completing this assignment, you will be able to:

  1. Explain how organizational culture and change-management methods affect information-system adoption.
  2. Evaluate a real organizational change effort using a reliable business source.
  3. Explain the roles of communication, training, leadership, and employee participation during implementation.
  4. Connect an information system to organizational processes, goals, and strategy.
  5. Investigate a contemporary information-systems, technology, data, or AI-related position.
  6. Identify technical and business skills needed for contemporary information-systems work.

Primary course learning-outcome alignment: CLO1, CLO9, CLO11, and CLO12.

Questions 1 through 5 require concise answers of no more than 500 characters. Respond directly to every part of each question. The Final Assignment Reflection allows a longer response.

Exercise 1-Organizational Change

Organizations must change or risk becoming obsolete. Choose one organizational-change method: innovation, reengineering, continuous improvement, outsourcing, offshoring, or downsizing. Then investigate how a specific organization used that method to accomplish change. The effort may have succeeded or failed; in either case, consider what the organization learned.
