Assignment Purpose and Learning Outcomes

The goal of this assignment is to connect the basic definition of management information systems with current organizational and societal uses of data, connected devices, and AI infrastructure.

After completing this assignment, you will be able to:

  1. Explain how people, processes, data, and technology work together in a management information system.
  2. Communicate professionally with a classmate while protecting personal privacy.
  3. Explain how Internet of Things devices, large data sets, and AI infrastructure affect organizations.
  4. Evaluate technological, social, workforce, environmental, or ethical implications of emerging technologies.
  5. Investigate how an organization generates and uses data to support operations or decisions.
  6. Communicate an evidence-based conclusion about an organizational or societal technology impact.

Primary course learning-outcome alignment: CLO1, CLO3, CLO7, CLO10, CLO11, and CLO12.

Questions 1 through 10 require concise answers of no more than 500 characters. Respond directly to every part of each question. The Final Assignment Reflection allows a longer response.
